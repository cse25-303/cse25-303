ACCESS CONTROL

We provide access control services to our customers to control who enters their premises. We use keypad systems, biometric systems, and keycard devices to enhance the security of customer premises and goods.

The access control service is integrated with CCTV cameras to provide evidence of who entered the premises. In case of a fire emergency, our systems automatically unlock all locked doors to protect lives.
