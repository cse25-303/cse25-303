ALARM SERVICE

Monameng Security Company provides its customers with a reliable alarm service. These alarms are monitored and maintained by the company at all times.

Our alarms are integrated with CCTV and armed response services to help protect our customers and their property in a fast and reliable way. These high-level alarms can detect intrusion and fire, and can also be activated in case of an emergency. Examples of alarms offered include smoke detectors, carbon monoxide alarms, panic alarms, and intrusion alarms.
