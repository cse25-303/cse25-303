WHO WE ARE

Monameng Security Company was founded in 1999 by Oteng Monameng, who has security industry experience of more than 10 years, and is now a leading high-tech security company dedicated to protecting lives and assets through innovation.

WHAT WE DO

We specialize in precision CCTV installation, state-of-the-art alarm systems, access control, and elite tactical armed response. With a focus on technical excellence and rapid intervention, we ensure clients are always one step ahead of potential threats.

MISSION

To deliver peace of mind through rapid tactical response and state-of-the-art electronic security. We strive to lead the industry by integrating innovative technology with elite response teams to ensure every alarm is met with immediate, effective action.

VALUES

  • Speed: Rapid armed intervention when it matters most.
  • Precision: Expert installation of CCTV and access control.
  • Vigilance: Constant alarm monitoring and technical support.
  • Reliability: High-end hardware and dedicated response units.
